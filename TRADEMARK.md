# Name and trademark

Pervigil is an unofficial fork of [Keepresso](https://github.com/gyorgysh/keepresso).
It is not related to, affiliated with, or endorsed by the official Keepresso
project or pueev OÜ.

## Upstream

The source code is free software under the [GNU GPL v3.0](LICENSE), inherited
from Keepresso, and Pervigil keeps the copyright and license notices the GPL
requires. The license covers the code. It does not grant rights to the
Keepresso name or brand: pueev OÜ holds the rights to the **Keepresso** name,
the **keepresso.com** domain, and the Keepresso logo and app icon.

Following the upstream trademark policy, Pervigil:

- uses its own name and its own mark and app icon (`docs/assets/brand`),
- uses its own identifiers (`io.github.bubb4rd.pervigil` and its helper,
  widget, and app group ids) and its own Sparkle feed, so it never collides
  with or updates over the official app,
- says it is unofficial in its README and About window.

## Forks of Pervigil

You are welcome to fork Pervigil under the GPL. If you offer your version to
others, give it a name and icon of its own, change the bundle identifiers and
the Sparkle feed (`SUFeedURL`), and say what it is derived from.
