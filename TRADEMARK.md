## Forks for your own use or for contributing

A GitHub fork, a local build for yourself, or a branch you send back as a pull
request is fine as is. You don't need to rename anything as long as you don't
offer it to the public.

## Redistributed builds, fan projects, and third-party apps

The rules below apply once you offer your version to others: a published
download, an app store listing, a package in a package manager, a hosted
service, or a project presented as its own app. You are welcome to do that
with the code. If you do:

- **Do not call it Keepresso.** Pick a name that does not contain "Keepresso"
  and cannot be confused with it.
- **Mark it as unofficial.** Say clearly, in its README, store or download page,
  and About screen, what it is, for example "an unofficial fork of Keepresso"
  or "a fan project based on Keepresso", and that it is not related to,
  affiliated with, or endorsed by the official Keepresso project or pueev OÜ.
- **Use your own branding.** Do not use the Keepresso logo, app icon, or a
  domain containing "keepresso".
- **Use your own identifiers.** Change the bundle identifier
  (`sh.gyorgy.keepresso` and its helper, widget, and app group ids) and point
  the Sparkle feed (`SUFeedURL`) at your own appcast, so your build never
  collides with or updates over the official app.

You may keep the copyright and license notices the GPL requires, and you may
state factually that your project is derived from the Keepresso source code.
